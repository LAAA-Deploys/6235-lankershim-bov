Executive Summary

The subject is a rare 4-parcel assemblage under common family ownership on the west side of Lankershim Blvd between Victory Blvd and Oxnard St in North Hollywood: the operating 20-room Silver Saddle Motel on two parcels, the adjacent free-standing restaurant building (vacant), and a large vacant land parcel behind it running back toward Colfax Ave. Together the four parcels total 79,728 SF (1.83 acres) of TOC Tier 1 land in one of the San Fernando Valley's most active redevelopment corridors.

This analysis values the assemblage as a single development offering, priced against the Valley's most recent land sales and buildable-unit benchmarks, with the operating motel providing income while a buyer entitles the site. The five development pathways below, from by-right through 100% affordable, define the buyer pools the offering will be marketed to.

Component 1: The Silver Saddle Motel (6229-6235 Lankershim)

A 20-room economy motel on two parcels totaling a full acre (43,578 SF) of Lankershim Blvd frontage. The 1940 main building (8,294 SF) and the 1924 two-building bungalow motel (3,122 SF) total 11,416 SF of improvements. The motel operates today, providing holding income while a buyer entitles the site or repositions the rooms.

Component 2: The Restaurant and Rear Development Land (6223 Lankershim)

A 1,856 SF free-standing former restaurant on the Lankershim frontage plus a 22,116 SF vacant parcel behind it, together 36,150 SF (0.83 acres) of development land. The restaurant has been vacant, and ownership has indicated interest in doing something with this portion of the site.

Location

The site sits on Lankershim Blvd, the spine of North Hollywood, about a mile north of the NoHo Arts District and the Metro B (Red) Line terminus, with the NoHo West mixed-use development and the 170 Freeway minutes away. The corridor has drawn sustained developer demand: the most recent land comparable in this analysis closed on Lankershim Blvd itself, less than half a mile north of the subject, in April 2026.

How to read these: five buyer pools for the same land. By-right buyers pay the most per unit for the fewest units; TOC, AB 1287, and MIIP buyers spread the basis across two to three times the count with mixed-income set-asides; AHIP buyers maximize the unit count with 100% affordable projects. The strongest offer typically comes from whichever pool is most active at the time of marketing. Key Takeaways

1Every comparable closed within the last year (September 2025 to April 2026). The four conventional sales averaged $4.71M per acre, and April 2026's Lankershim Blvd sale, half a mile from the subject, printed $112.32 per SF of land, essentially the subject's $112.88 per SF guidance basis for the full assemblage. The subject's $4.92M per acre sits inside the band set by the two closest-in prints (Lankershim $4.89M and Vanowen $5.93M per acre).
2Affordable and density-bonus programs set the pace: four of the five land comps carried buildable-unit underwriting, from $9,400 per unit on the one lender disposition to $37,900 per unit on Meta Housing's Tampa Ave buy. At the $9M guidance the subject prices at roughly $33,200 per AB 1287 unit and $20,700 per AHIP (ED1) unit, inside the range those buyers have paid across the Valley.
3All four parcels sit under common family ownership: a contiguous 79,728 SF (1.83 acre) TOC Tier 1 assemblage on Lankershim Blvd, an offering size that essentially never reaches this corridor in one transaction.
4The components stand alone: the motel can sell to a hospitality operator while ownership develops the land, or the entire assemblage can go to a single developer as one of the largest sites in North Hollywood.
5Active supply moves slowly at aspirational pricing: the one comparable large-site listing (14920 Sherman Way, Van Nuys, 2.44 acres asking $93.61 per SF) has sat 16 months, and the two Reseda sales took 610 and 721 days to clear. Realistic day-one pricing is what separates the sales from the sitters.
6Opportunity Zone, again: the subject sits in federally designated Opportunity Zone census tract 1241.04, and the tract has been renominated for the renewed Opportunity Zone 2.0 program, extending the capital gains deferral and basis step-up incentives for a developer or long-hold buyer. Verify on the California OZ 2.0 nomination map.

Marketing Approach

We would market the property first as one development offering across all four parcels, the full 79,728 SF (1.83 acre) site. In parallel, we would also offer the Silver Saddle Motel and the restaurant plus rear land as individual sales for buyers who do not need to place the entire amount, including 1031 exchange buyers matching a smaller requirement. Smaller purchase prices open the offering to a much deeper pool of buyers, and the property sells whichever way produces the strongest total outcome, whole or in parts.
